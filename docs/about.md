**CareCall** is a web-based platform designed to automate outbound medical appointment scheduling using **AI voice assistants**. 

By integrating **Telnyx** for telecommunications and **Supabase** for data management, the system allows clinic staff to launch targeted campaigns for services like wellness visits or vaccinations. 

The **AI assistant** handles live conversations, verifies patient identities, and offers real-time booking slots while ensuring data integrity through server-side validation.

**Security and compliance** are central to the architecture, featuring strict identity checks, answering machine detection, and protocols to protect patient health information.

Beyond simple scheduling, the framework functions as a **versatile campaign engine** capable of supporting future outreach efforts such as medication reminders or intake surveys.
